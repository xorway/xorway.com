About Xorway Solutions

Xorway Solutions is a small, independent software company. We build high-quality software — and we care rather a lot about how it is built, and about the freedom of the people who use it.

The company has been “empowering the world with high-quality software solutions since 1995”. If you have visited our front page, you already know that we secretly believe it is still 1995 — back when software was still distributed on floppy disks, Windows 95 was released with its iconic startup sound, and a program you owned was a program you could actually understand, inspect and repair. That spirit still guides how we work today.

What we do

We design and build software for people who need it to be dependable: clear code, sensible architecture, and a strong preference for open, long-lived technologies over the fashion of the week. We would rather ship something small that looks and feels great than something large that feels half-finished. In that sense, we believe that LLMs ("AI"), if used unwisely, can cause great harm: they accelerate the mechanical production of software ("generating code"), but they don't necessarily increase the overall quality of the finished product. We are not strictly anti-LLM, but we do believe that they should be applied with precision and sensible skepticism.

Free software at heart

We think software freedom is not a side quest but the point. Whenever we can, we build on free ("open-source") software, and we contribute back. If you would like the longer argument, see Why Free Software Matters.

Perlang

Xorway Solutions backs Perlang, a free programming language. It is a good example of what we care about: useful software that stays free, funded in a way that keeps it that way.
