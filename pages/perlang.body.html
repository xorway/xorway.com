Perlang

Perlang is a free software ("open source") programming language, backed by Xorway Solutions. This page is the short version — the full story lives on the dedicated Perlang site.

What is Perlang?

Perlang is a programming language project with a simple ambition: to be genuinely useful while staying genuinely free. It is released under the LGPL and MIT licences, so anyone can use it, study it, and build on it. You can follow the open-source project at perlang.org.

Its connection to Xorway

Xorway Solutions backs Perlang and helps fund its development. We keep two things deliberately separate: Perlang the free software project, and the commercial side that helps pay for it. Perlang stays free for everyone — the commercial side simply gives those who want to support that freedom a way to do so. It is the free-software model we believe in, put into practice.
